Wingfoil & Weather

A weather page for wingfoilers in Trieste

The itch

Trieste is a wind city — Bora, Scirocco, thermal breezes. As a wingfoiler you spend half your life refreshing forecasts to guess if it's worth rigging up. So I built a small page that pulls the data that actually matters and shows it at a glance.

What it does

  • Current wind speed, gusts and direction for the Gulf of Trieste.
  • A short-term forecast tuned for the spots I ride.
  • No clutter — just "go / no-go" at a glance.
