Logistic Manager

Marina Militare Nastro Rosa Tour · Jun 2020 — Nov 2021

Context

The Marina Militare Nastro Rosa Tour is an itinerant sailing event circumnavigating Italy, with a travelling village that sets up and tears down at each stopover. I ran the logistics of moving that village around the country.

What I did

  • Planned and coordinated logistics for 8 stopovers in under 30 days.
  • Managed transport, setup and teardown of the itinerant village at each port.
  • Coordinated suppliers, local authorities and on-site crews.
  • Handled sponsorship management for the tour.

Outcome

  • 8 cities, <30 days — delivered on a tight, moving schedule.
  • Smooth handovers between stopovers with no event downtime.

Skills

  • Logistics
  • Event management
  • Supplier coordination
  • Sponsorship
