Sports Director

Barcolana · Oct 2018 — Dec 2020

Context

Barcolana, held every October in the Gulf of Trieste, is the largest sailing regatta in the world. As Sports Director I owned the sporting side of an event with thousands of boats on a single start line.

What I did

  • Ran the software-based registration for 2,500+ boats.
  • Managed permits, entry validation and crew documentation.
  • Coordinated on-water management, start procedures and safety.
  • Liaised with the race committee, authorities and competitors.

Outcome

  • 2,500+ boats registered and managed through one digital flow.
  • A smooth start and on-water operation at world-record scale.

Skills

  • Event management
  • Registration software
  • On-water operations
  • Stakeholder coordination
