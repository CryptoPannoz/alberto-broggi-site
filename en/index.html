2026–nowNoura TrainingCRM development600+ clients · 15 trainers

Noura is a personal training studio in Trieste with over 600 clients and 15 trainers. I designed and built their custom CRM: one system where trainers, front office and management follow every client, from the first assessment to the training programme. It has been in production since October 2026 and the whole staff uses it every day.

Screenshots with sample data: no real client data

What I do

  • Building the CRM: client profile, medical history, assessment, training programmes and notes, with different views and permissions for trainers, front office, management and external professionals.
  • Medical certificates: a digital archive per client, automatic reading of the expiry date, alerts and reminders when a certificate is missing or expired.
  • Client relationships: schedule synced with the booking system, follow-ups, internal messaging between trainers and action lists to keep clients who are about to drop out.
  • Migration of the history from old spreadsheets and the previous app, with backups and data continuity.

Results

  • Over 600 client profiles in one reliable source of truth instead of scattered spreadsheets.
  • Medical certificates under control: anyone not up to date stands out immediately.
  • Every trainer working in the CRM since the October 2026 launch.

Tech & tools

  • Next.js
  • Postgres
  • Vercel
  • API integration
  • OCR
  • Roles & permissions
